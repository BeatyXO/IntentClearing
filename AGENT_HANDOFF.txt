PRODUCT BOUNDARY

IntentClearing is a standalone reusable Intelligent Contract primitive, not a full application. There must be no frontend.

The primitive provides batch clearing for markets whose orders contain qualitative requirements that cannot be reduced to ordinary numeric price/quantity fields.

Its defining architecture is:

- a market owner creates a bounded market-local semantic attribute dictionary;
- the dictionary is sealed and pinned by a non-empty definition_hash;
- each order is profiled INDEPENDENTLY against that sealed dictionary;
- the AI/LLM must never compare request A to offer B;
- the AI/LLM must never choose a counterparty, winner, clearing priority, price, fill quantity, or settlement result;
- REQUEST profiles contain required_mask, forbidden_mask, preferred_mask, and ambiguous_mask;
- OFFER profiles contain present_mask, absent_mask, and ambiguous_mask;
- validators independently re-profile the same single order and equivalence is based only on bounded semantic masks, not free-form rationale;
- explicit numeric fields such as quantity and unit price remain deterministic user inputs and must never be inferred by the LLM;
- deterministic_compatible performs all request/offer compatibility checks after profiling;
- ambiguity must fail closed where it affects required or forbidden compatibility;
- every active order in a frozen epoch must be assessed before clearing, preventing selective clearing around unassessed competitors;
- the epoch freezes the active order set into epoch_hash;
- clearing is deterministic from stored profiles, explicit numeric terms, and stable tie-break rules;
- requests rank by maximum unit price descending, then submission time, then order ID;
- compatible offers rank per request by preference score descending, ask price ascending, submission time, then order ID;
- trade price is the offer ask;
- quantities may partially fill;
- every fill receives fill_hash and pins market_hash + epoch_hash;
- settlement_root deterministically folds the produced fill hashes;
- ClearingGate proves typed IC-to-IC reuse and must bind exact market_hash, epoch_hash, fill_hash, caller party membership, and one-time consumption/replay protection.

Do NOT collapse this design into any of the following:

- “AI decides who matches”;
- pairwise semantic comparison between request and offer descriptions;
- a generic LLM marketplace;
- a semantic scheduler or lock manager;
- a simple storage contract with an LLM wrapper;
- a frontend product.

The architectural distinction matters. The consensus layer produces reusable ONE-ORDER semantic profiles. The market clearing layer is deterministic.
